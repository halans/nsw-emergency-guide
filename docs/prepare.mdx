Get Ready NSW organises preparation around **understanding your risk**, **making an emergency plan** and **packing an emergency kit**. Use the official guidance for the hazard, location and needs of your household.

## Discuss these needs before an emergency

- Who needs help, and who can provide it if the usual carer is unavailable?
- Where will you go, how will you travel, and what is your backup route?
- How will you receive warnings and contact people if power or mobile service fails?
- What medicines, documents, equipment and supplies need to leave with you?
- How will you move pets, livestock or assistance animals safely?

These are planning prompts, not an evacuation order or a complete kit specification. Use the agency checklists below and review your plan when your circumstances change.

## Other states and territories

In an emergency anywhere in Australia, call 000. These are each state's own services; check the official page before calling.

- **VIC** — State emergency service: [Victoria State Emergency Service (VICSES)](https://www.ses.vic.gov.au/en/about-us/contact-us) — [132 500 — VICSES flood and storm help](tel:132500)
- **VIC** — Fire and rescue: [Country Fire Authority (CFA)](https://www.cfa.vic.gov.au/)
- **VIC** — Fire and rescue: [Fire Rescue Victoria (FRV)](https://frv.vic.gov.au/contact-us)
- **QLD** — State emergency service: [Queensland State Emergency Service (Queensland SES)](https://www.ses.qld.gov.au/) — [132 500 — Queensland SES](tel:132500)
- **QLD** — Fire and rescue: [Queensland Fire Department (QFD)](https://www.qld.gov.au/emergency/emergencies-services/response/fire-rescue)
- **WA** — State emergency service: [WA State Emergency Service (SES) emergency assistance (DFES)](https://www.dfes.wa.gov.au/contact) — [132 500 — SES emergency assistance](tel:132500)
