### NSW Mental Health Line

<a className="phone-link" href="tel:1800011511">1800 011 511</a>

Free statewide advice, triage and connection to NSW Health mental health services; a trained professional completes a brief assessment and recommends appropriate care.

**Hours:** 24/7

<div className="resource-caution">

**Important:** Not a one-off counselling service. If someone is in immediate danger, call 000 or attend an emergency department.

</div>

For another language, call TIS National 131 450 and ask to be connected. Relay options are listed on the source page.

**Device safety:** calls, messages and browsing may leave traces. This site does not have a quick-exit feature and cannot clear browser history or records on a phone bill. Use a safer device if possible and follow the service’s own safety guidance.

## Language and relay access

See [accessible and language support](/accessible-help). For the NSW Mental Health Line, NSW Health lists a TIS National pathway on **131 450**.

## Other states and territories

- **VIC** — Mental-health crisis line: [Victorian Department of Health: mental health telephone and online services](https://www.health.vic.gov.au/mental-health-services/telephone-and-online-services)
- **QLD** — Mental-health crisis line: [1300 MH CALL (Queensland mental health access line)](https://www.qld.gov.au/health/mental-health/help-lines/1300-mh-call) — [1300 642 255](tel:1300642255)
- **WA** — Mental-health crisis line: [Mental Health Emergency Response Line (MHERL)](https://mhc.wa.gov.au/getting-help/helplines/mental-health-emergency-response-line) — [1300 555 788](tel:1300555788)
- **ACT** — Mental-health crisis line: [Access Mental Health Line (Canberra Health Services)](https://www.canberrahealthservices.act.gov.au/services-and-clinics/services/access-mental-health) — [1800 629 354](tel:1800629354)
