### NSW Mental Health Line

<a className="phone-link" href="tel:1800011511">1800 011 511</a>

Free statewide advice, triage and connection to NSW Health mental health services; a trained professional completes a brief assessment and recommends appropriate care.

**Hours:** 24/7

<div className="resource-caution">

**Important:** Not a one-off counselling service. If someone is in immediate danger, call 000 or attend an emergency department.

</div>

For another language, call TIS National 131 450 and ask to be connected. Relay options are listed on the source page.

## Domestic, family or sexual violence

<div className="resource-list">

<section className="resource-card" id="resource-respect">
<div className="resource-meta"><span>Australia</span><span>Reviewed 14 Oct 2026</span></div>

### 1800RESPECT

<a className="phone-link" href="tel:1800737732">1800 737 732</a>

National counselling, information and support for people impacted by domestic, family or sexual violence. Text: 0458 737 732; online chat and video call are also offered.

**Hours:** 24/7

<div className="resource-caution">

**Important:** If in immediate danger, call 000. Use a safer device if possible: calls, texts and browsing can leave records. This directory has no quick-exit feature and does not erase browsing history.

</div>

<a className="source-link" href="https://1800respect.org.au/calling-1800respect">Open official resource — 1800RESPECT ↗</a>

</section>

</div>

**Device safety:** calls, messages and browsing may leave traces. This site does not have a quick-exit feature and cannot clear browser history or records on a phone bill. Use a safer device if possible and follow the service’s own safety guidance.
