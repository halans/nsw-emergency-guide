## Purpose and scope

Emergency Ready brings together Australian national contacts and NSW emergency resources. NSW-specific numbers, planning resources and recovery pathways are labelled. It is not a complete directory of every state, council or utility service.

## Important limitations

- This site is **independent** and is not operated or endorsed by government or the linked services.
- It does not receive emergency requests, dispatch help, provide medical/legal advice or assess whether a route or location is safe.
- It does not display live warnings. Use official current sources and emergency-service directions.
- Links, hours, eligibility and service arrangements can change after the review date.
- For immediate danger or a life-threatening emergency, call **000**.

## Privacy and safety

The site has no analytics, advertising, account signup, embedded third-party maps or AI advice assistant. Search runs locally in your browser after its index loads. Fonts are hosted with the site. Hosting providers can still process ordinary request logs, and external resources have their own privacy policies.

Your browser may remember visits and preferences, and device/network records may remain. This is **not** a private browsing or quick-exit tool. See [personal safety considerations](/crisis-support).

## Offline and printing

Important content is static HTML and can be read with JavaScript disabled. Search and mobile drawer interactions need JavaScript; an always-visible page directory is provided below for a no-script fallback. The deployed site does not install a service worker or promise offline access. Save or print essential details in advance and open current official warnings when connected.

## Corrections and maintenance

To report a wrong, missing or out-of-date contact or link, open an issue on the project’s [GitHub repository](https://github.com/halans/nsw-emergency-guide/issues). Do not include personal or sensitive information, and do not use GitHub to ask for emergency help: call **000**. The site itself has no backend or reporting inbox. Maintainers can edit the resource register and page templates, run the documented checks and rebuild.
