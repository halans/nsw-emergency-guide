### Temporary and emergency housing / Link2Home

<a className="phone-link" href="tel:1800152152">1800 152 152 — Link2Home</a>

Link2Home helps people who are homeless or at risk of homelessness. For disaster accommodation pathways, contact Service NSW on 13 77 88 or a recovery centre.

**Hours:** Link2Home: 24/7

<a className="source-link" href="https://www.nsw.gov.au/emergency/recovery/temporary-housing">Open official resource — Temporary and emergency housing / Link2Home ↗</a>

## Legal support after a disaster

<div className="resource-list">

<section className="resource-card" id="resource-legal">
<div className="resource-meta"><span>NSW</span><span>Reviewed 14 Oct 2026</span></div>

### Legal Aid NSW — Disaster Response Legal Service

<a className="phone-link" href="tel:1800801529">1800 801 529</a>

Free legal help after bushfire, storm or flood: insurance claims/disputes, tenancy/housing, disaster grants, financial hardship, Centrelink, work rights and documents.

**Hours:** Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm

<a className="source-link" href="https://www.legalaid.nsw.gov.au/my-problem-is-about/disasters/disaster-response-legal-service">Open official resource — Legal Aid NSW — Disaster Response Legal Service ↗</a>

</section>

</div>

Keep relevant documents where safe to do so and seek advice suited to your circumstances. This directory does not provide legal advice.

## Emotional support

Recovery can be difficult long after the immediate danger has passed. [Crisis and mental-health support](/crisis-support) is available alongside practical assistance.
