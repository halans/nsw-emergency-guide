## Find guidance for your situation

The official hub links to guidance for:

- Pets, backyard chickens and other birds.
- Wildlife and wildlife carers.
- Poultry and livestock farmers, and vets.
- First Nations communities.
- Human health, translated resources and local state/territory advice.

Use the [Australian Government bird flu hub](https://birdflu.gov.au/) for those pathways. It redirects to [DAFF’s H5 campaign](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/campaigns/birdflu).

## Current status belongs on the official source

Outbreak status, restrictions and reporting data change. This directory deliberately does **not** reproduce case totals, declare an area disease-free or provide animal-handling or treatment instructions. Check current local advice through the official hub.

For a human medical emergency, call **000**. For non-emergency health advice, [healthdirect: 1800 022 222](tel:1800022222).

## Animal evacuation planning is a separate task

[Plan for pets, livestock and assistance animals](/animals) before an evacuation. Do not transport potentially infected wildlife or animals on the basis of this directory; ask the reporting service or a vet for situation-specific directions.
