## How this directory was checked

Resource names, contact details and summaries were reviewed against government, emergency-service or service-provider pages on **14 October 2026**. Hours appear only where supported by the cited research. A review date is not a guarantee of continuing accuracy or current service availability.

Each entry links directly to its official source. MyFirePlan, NSW RFS, HazardWatch and birdflu.gov.au are included explicitly. The bird flu short address redirects to DAFF’s H5 campaign.

The source project includes machine-readable resource data, research notes and test reports. You can also download the [resource register as JSON](/resources.json).

### Emergency Relay Service / National Relay Service (NRS)

<strong className="phone-link">106 — TTY/TDD only</strong>

Accessible emergency-call pathways for people who are d/Deaf or have hearing or speech difficulty. Use 106 from a TTY/TDD; it is not a general text-message number.

**Hours:** NRS is available 24/7 except Video Relay; Video Relay hours are limited.

<div className="resource-caution">

**Important:** Video Relay is not recommended for emergency calls: it cannot be identified/prioritised as emergency and operates only limited hours. Internet/mobile-based relay may be less reliable than 106 and may not identify caller/location. For a TTY, dial 106 first.

</div>
