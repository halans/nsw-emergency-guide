## Understand warning levels

The NSW emergency hub explains three levels: **Advice**, **Watch and Act**, and **Emergency Warning**. Read the action statement, not just the colour or level. Different hazards and locations require different actions.

Source: [NSW Government — emergencies and warning information](https://www.nsw.gov.au/emergency).

## Do not rely on one channel

Use relevant agency websites and apps and local emergency broadcasts. Power, mobile coverage and internet access can fail. Follow [Get Ready NSW](https://www.nsw.gov.au/emergency/get-ready) to prepare communication backups.
