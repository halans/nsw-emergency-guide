For an immediate or life-threatening emergency, call **000**. Ask for Police, Fire or Ambulance, give your exact location and follow the call-taker’s instructions. Do not delay an emergency call to browse this directory.

## Police assistance and health advice

These are not substitutes for emergency response. If someone is seriously ill or injured, or a crime is happening now, call **000**.

## If a voice call is not accessible

[Accessible emergency calls and relay options](/accessible-help) explains 106 and links to the National Relay Service. **106 is for TTY/TDD emergency calls, not ordinary SMS.**

## Crisis support

For emotional distress, mental-health support or family/sexual violence support, see [crisis and personal safety](/crisis-support). If someone’s life is in immediate danger, call **000**.

## Other states and territories

In an emergency anywhere in Australia, call 000. These are each state's own services; check the official page before calling.

- **VIC** — State emergency service: [Victoria State Emergency Service (VICSES)](https://www.ses.vic.gov.au/en/about-us/contact-us) — [132 500 — VICSES flood and storm help](tel:132500)
- **VIC** — Police assistance (non-emergency): [Victoria Police Assistance Line](https://www.police.vic.gov.au/contact) — [131 444 — Victoria Police Assistance Line](tel:131444)
- **QLD** — State emergency service: [Queensland State Emergency Service (Queensland SES)](https://www.ses.qld.gov.au/) — [132 500 — Queensland SES](tel:132500)
- **QLD** — Police assistance (non-emergency): [Policelink (Queensland Police non-urgent reporting and enquiries)](https://www.qld.gov.au/youth/legal-support-and-your-rights/police-services-crime-prevention) — [131 444 — Policelink](tel:131444)
- **WA** — State emergency service: [WA State Emergency Service (SES) emergency assistance (DFES)](https://www.dfes.wa.gov.au/contact) — [132 500 — SES emergency assistance](tel:132500)
- **WA** — Police assistance (non-emergency): [WA Police Force — Contact WA Police (131 444)](https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/western-australia-police-force/contact-wa-police) — [131 444 — WA Police non-emergency](tel:131444)
- **SA** — State emergency service: [South Australian State Emergency Service (SA SES)](https://ses.sa.gov.au/about-us/contact-us) — [132 500 — SA SES](tel:132500)
- **TAS** — State emergency service: [Tasmania State Emergency Service (Tasmania SES)](https://www.ses.tas.gov.au/about/contact-us/) — [132 500 — Tasmania SES (flood, storm, tsunami)](tel:132500)
- **TAS** — Police assistance (non-emergency): [Tasmania Police: Police Assistance Line](https://www.police.tas.gov.au/contact-us/) — [131 444 — Tasmania Police Assistance Line](tel:131444)
- **ACT** — State emergency service: [ACT State Emergency Service (ACT SES)](https://esa.act.gov.au/about-esa-emergency-services/state-emergency-service/contact-ses) — [132 500 — ACT SES storm and flood assistance](tel:132500)
- **ACT** — Police assistance (non-emergency): [ACT Policing: Police Assistance Line](https://www.afp.gov.au/about-us/contact-us) — [131 444 — ACT Policing Police Assistance Line](tel:131444)
- **NT** — State emergency service: [NT Emergency Service (NTES)](https://pfes.nt.gov.au/nt-emergency-service-general-enquiries) — [132 500 — NTES (floods, storms, cyclones)](tel:132500)
- **NT** — Police assistance (non-emergency): [NT Police Force: police contacts](https://pfes.nt.gov.au/online-services/police-contacts) — [131 444 — NT Police (non-emergency)](tel:131444)
