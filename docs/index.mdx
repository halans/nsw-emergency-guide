## Four useful starting points

<div className="resource-list">

<section className="resource-card" id="resource-myfireplan">
<div className="resource-meta"><span>NSW</span><span>Reviewed 14 Oct 2026</span></div>

### MyFirePlan

NSW RFS online bushfire planning tool. Discuss your household’s leaving triggers, safer destination, routes and what to take before fire threatens.

<div className="resource-caution">

**Important:** Leaving early is your safest choice. Do not wait for an official warning to decide when to leave. This tool is for planning, not incident warnings.

</div>

<a className="source-link" href="https://www.myfireplan.com.au/">Open official resource — MyFirePlan ↗</a>

</section>

<section className="resource-card" id="resource-hazardwatch">
<div className="resource-meta"><span>NSW</span><span>Reviewed 14 Oct 2026</span></div>

### HazardWatch

Official interactive hazard-warning map. NSW SES links to HazardWatch for current flood, severe weather and tsunami warnings.

<div className="resource-caution">

**Important:** Check the displayed hazard coverage, location, issue time and full warning. A blank map or no displayed warnings does not mean your location is safe.

</div>

<a className="source-link" href="https://www.hazardwatch.gov.au/">Open official resource — HazardWatch ↗</a>

</section>

<div className="resource-meta"><span>Australia</span><span>Reviewed 14 Oct 2026</span></div>

### H5 bird flu — Australian Government

<a className="phone-link" href="tel:1800675888">1800 675 888 — Emergency Animal Disease Hotline</a>

Australian Government H5 bird flu hub: local advice, reporting, wildlife, pets, backyard birds, farms, human health and translated resources. The short address redirects to the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry campaign.

**Hours:** 24/7

<div className="resource-caution">

**Important:** If you notice sick or dead birds or other animals, do not touch them or get too close. Take photos only if safe, note the location and report it. This is animal-disease reporting, not a human medical emergency line.

</div>

<a className="source-link" href="https://birdflu.gov.au/">Open official resource — H5 bird flu — Australian Government ↗</a>

</section>
